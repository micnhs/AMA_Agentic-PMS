## Context

The Goal Card named a 5-level pilot scale; its source could not be confirmed.

## Options

- 5 levels (pilot).
- 4 levels according to MDCG 2020-10/1 Rev. 1, section 9.

## Decision

Decided on 10 Nov 2026 (Q-2): **4 levels – not related, possible, probable, causal.**
"Not assessable" means *possible*.

## Consequences

L07 and L11 were relabelled (concept section 11.3). The ranges remain "not clinically validated". The
guideline applies to clinical investigations; the demo uses it by analogy (A-13).

| Letter | Label | v0.1 | v0.2 and later | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L07 | causality_range | not related – unlikely | not related – possible | "unlikely" no longer exists. According to MDCG 2020-10/1, "possible" applies when a relationship cannot be fully ruled out; the range covers both neighbouring levels of the dropped level. |
| L11 | causality_range | unlikely – possible | not related – possible | Same rule: the dropped lower bound "unlikely" is replaced by the next lower level. |

Source: MDCG 2020-10/1 Rev. 1, section 9 (`md_mdcg_2020-10-1_guidance_safety_reporting_en.pdf`, EC
download, PDF p. 10-11; source ID `MDCG-2020-10-1-S9`) and decision Q-2 (`DEC-Q2`).

## Implementation in this repository (not part of the concept text)

- Agent A3 returns a draft causality rating on this scale; every AI output is labelled "draft" and the
  ranges are shown as "not clinically validated".
- The causality range is an AI field. The offline golden run does not compare it; it is checked against
  the live model in Gate C (test T30).
