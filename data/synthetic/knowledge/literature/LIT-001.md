## Summary

Case series of nine patients with leaflet tears within twelve weeks of implantation of stented pericardial aortic bioprostheses at four demo centres. Early tears were rare compared with late structural valve deterioration. In six of nine cases the explant analysis found leaflet thickness at the lower end of the specification or handling marks. The authors recommend a lot-level review when early tears cluster. A case series cannot establish how often this happens.
