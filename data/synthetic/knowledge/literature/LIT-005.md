## Summary

In this cohort most clinical valve thromboses occurred with subtherapeutic anticoagulation or after the patient interrupted treatment. Gradients usually returned to normal under anticoagulation. Valve thrombosis is a recognised risk of bioprostheses; adherence is the main factor that can be changed.
