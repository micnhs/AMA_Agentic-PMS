## Summary

Descriptive analysis of forty-one stem fractures. Fractures occurred at a median of fourteen months after implantation. Reported risk factors were a body mass index above 35, small stem sizes combined with high offset, and missing proximal bone support. Exposure data were incomplete, so no rates are reported.
