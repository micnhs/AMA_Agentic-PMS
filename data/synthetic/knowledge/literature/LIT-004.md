## Summary

Report of five liner dissociations. Contributing factors were incomplete seating of the liner, deformation of the locking ring and impingement of the femoral neck. Dissociation usually presented as a sudden painful clunk and needed revision. The authors recommend checking seating during surgery and reviewing the design margin of the locking mechanism when dissociations recur across lots.
