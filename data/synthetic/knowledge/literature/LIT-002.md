## Summary

Staphylococcus aureus prosthetic valve endocarditis carries a high mortality. Dental procedures without antibiotic prophylaxis and other sources of bacteraemia are the main patient-related risk factors. The reviewed studies did not show differences between types of bioprosthesis. Endocarditis is a known risk of all prosthetic valves.
